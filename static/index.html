Auditor Highlighting & Quality Rules

  • Priority 1 Control Mandate: Any process failure mode with Pred Priority Level == 1 must have robust controls where Pred Det ≤ 4 (Automated poka-yoke, automatic locking, or error proofing).
  • Violation Highlighting: If a row has Pred Priority Level == 1 but Pred Det > 4 (or invalid/missing), the cell is highlighted in Solid Yellow with Bold Red Text.
  • Design-to-Process (D-P) Gap Rule: When Severity is 9 or 10, Detection must be < 3; otherwise a critical gap flag is raised.

System Assumptions & Auditor Verification Scope

Critical operating assumptions, hierarchical PFMEA mappings (Manufacturing Step → Failure Mode → Effects / Causes / Controls), and AI verification boundaries.

Auditor Guidance
1

AI Verification Notice & Human Oversight

AI can make mistakes. Always cross-verify the output of this AI tool before making quality, safety, or manufacturing decisions.

AI recommendations serve strictly as assistive decision support. Final engineering approval, risk prioritization, and corrective action sign-offs remain the direct responsibility of the qualified PFMEA auditor and process engineering team.
2

Severity Score Hierarchical Mapping

Manufacturing Step → Failure Mode → Effect of Failure → Severity Score

Severity scores need to be mapped to the Potential Effect of Failure, which maps to the Potential Failure Mode, and back to the specific Manufacturing Step.

It is assumed that the potential failure mode and its resulting effects of failure for a given manufacturing process step are correct, accurate, and pre-verified by the auditor. Severity directly evaluates the seriousness of the effect on customer safety and plant operations.
3

Occurrence & Detection Hierarchical Mapping

Manufacturing Step → Failure Mode → Causes & Controls → Occ & Det Scores

Similarly, Occurrence and Detection scores must be mapped through the Manufacturing Step → Failure Mode → Potential Cause & Process Controls: Occurrence mapped to the frequency/likelihood of the cause, and Detection mapped to the control capability to detect the cause or failure mode at that step.

The numerical Occurrence (Occ) scores (based on process capability / statistical history) and baseline Detection (Det) scores entered in the workbook are assumed to have been verified and validated by the engineering auditor prior to audit.
4

AI Tool Scope of Verification

Effect & Control Text ↔ AIAG Standards Criteria → Audited Ratings & Gaps

The AI tool only facilitates verifying the Severity effect text and Detection control text against AIAG standards.

The automated auditor cross-examines textual descriptions against official AIAG 4th Edition criteria to identify over/under-ratings, verify D-P gap compliance (when Severity is 9–10), and flag risk priority violations, relying on the structural mappings above being established by the engineering team.
